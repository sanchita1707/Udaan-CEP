About Udaan

What is Udaan?

Udaan is a community-focused educational web platform created as part of a Community Engagement Program (CEP) by B.Sc. Computer Science students. The platform is designed to support children from NGOs and underprivileged backgrounds by providing free access to quality educational resources.

The name "Udaan" means "flight" in Hindi — symbolising the hope and opportunity that education gives every child to soar to new heights.

Our Mission & Vision

The values that guide everything we do at Udaan.

🎯

Our Mission

"To make learning simple, accessible, interactive and enjoyable for every child, regardless of their background or resources."

🌟

Our Vision

"To encourage every child to learn, explore and grow with confidence, building a brighter and more educated community."

Our Objectives

  • Provide simple and engaging educational resources for school-age children across multiple subjects.
  • Encourage children to learn through fun interactive activities like puzzles, memory games and math challenges.
  • Improve basic digital awareness and computer literacy through dedicated Computer Basics lessons.
  • Promote interactive learning through MCQ quizzes with immediate score feedback and progress tracking.
  • Support children from NGOs and community organisations through our College CEP initiative.
  • Maintain a safe, child-friendly and privacy-conscious educational environment at all times.

Our Journey

📋 Project Proposal

Udaan was conceived as a CEP project to address educational gaps for underprivileged children.

🎨 Design & Development

The platform was designed and developed by B.Sc. Computer Science students using modern web technologies.

🏫 NGO Partnership

We partnered with a local NGO to conduct educational sessions and activities for children.

🚀 Platform Launch

Udaan was launched as a free educational platform available to children and community members.

🌱 Ongoing Growth

Continuing to add more content, activities and features to support children's learning journey.
