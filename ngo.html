Our Community Engagement Program – Teaching children through love, patience and dedication.

Our NGO Initiative

As part of our college's Community Engagement Program (CEP), our team of B.Sc. Computer Science students partnered with a local NGO to conduct educational sessions for underprivileged children. Our goal was to bring the joy of learning directly to children who may not have regular access to quality educational resources.

We conducted interactive sessions covering English, Mathematics, Computer awareness and fun educational activities. The children participated enthusiastically and showed remarkable curiosity and willingness to learn.

Privacy Notice: To protect the privacy of the children we worked with, no personal information, names or identifying details of any child are shared on this page.
